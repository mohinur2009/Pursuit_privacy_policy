Pursuit

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

In short: Pursuit does not collect, store on a server, or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

About this app

Pursuit is a planner that helps students keep track of hackathons, internships, competitions, scholarships and other opportunities, put them in the best order, and track their results. Pursuit is developed by Mohinur Abdurasulova.

Information we collect

None. Pursuit has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no access to the internet. The developer cannot see anything you enter in the app.

Information stored on your device

The events, deadlines, results, your optional name and your reminder settings are saved only inside the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read them.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Pursuit asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are created on your phone from your own list. Nothing is sent to any server. You can turn reminders off at any time in the app's Settings or in your phone's settings.

Backups

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include Pursuit's data in your personal device backup. That backup is handled by Google under your account settings, and the developer cannot access it.

Sharing your information

Pursuit does not share, sell or transfer any data to anyone, because it does not collect any.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → Delete all my data removes every event and setting.
  • Uninstalling Pursuit also removes all of its data from your phone.

Children

Pursuit is intended for students aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
